Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Game World is a collection of games for children, made by an independent developer. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: it collects nothing.

Information we collect

None. Game World does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information or usage data. It has no accounts, no chat, no analytics and no advertising.

Information stored on your device

The app saves game progress (such as stars earned) and settings (sound and language) on your device only. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this data in your own Google account backup, which is handled by Google under its privacy policy. We never receive it.

Permissions

The app needs no special permissions and works offline.

Children

Game World is designed for children and follows Google Play's Families policy. Settings and any link out of the app are behind a parental check.

Changes

If the app ever adds ads or purchases, this policy will be updated before that release, and ads will be limited to child-safe, non-personalised ads from providers certified for Google Play's Families program.
